    List of partials

    These choose which measured partials are listed and how they are named. The measurement itself is always made the same way: 170 ms frames, windows of up to 5 s, chosen by the default criteria below.

    Note names and cents are relative to 12-tone equal temperament at this A4.
    Only partials at or above this confidence are listed and marked (default 70 %).
    Level below the strongest partial in the window (default −60 dB).
    Partials heard for less time than this are left out (default 1 s). Off lists every confident partial, however short.

    Observe (real time)

    These change only the real-time view and its list; measurements are not affected. The list shows partials that have sounded for about ¾ s, within 40 dB of the strongest (less if Weakest partial listed is set lower), and is renewed every second.

    Shorter frames follow fast changes; longer frames separate partials that lie close together, especially in the bass, but dim partials whose pitch moves quickly, such as the upper harmonics of a voice with vibrato.
    How quickly the display follows the sound. With medium frames a partial shows in full about 80 ms (fast), 100 ms (medium) or 160 ms (steady) after it starts, and is gone about as long after it stops.

    Display

    Half-width of the strongest lobes on the spiral, in cents. Display only; the measured frequencies are unaffected.
